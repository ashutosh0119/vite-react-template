Transparency & Standards

Terms of Service & Platform Policies

Clear, honest policies powered by SehatGraph Technologies Private Limited.

school Course Access & Mentorship Support

Enrolled students receive unlimited lifetime access to all recorded lecture modules, future curriculum updates, downloadable architectural blueprints, and resource materials. Dedicated 1-on-1 mentorship and doubt-clearing sessions remain active for 6 months from enrollment.

gavel Educational & Placement Disclaimer

Pragvix Learn is an educational training platform. While we provide verified startup internship listings, career mentorship, resume reviews, and direct interview opportunities through our startup partner network, we do not guarantee job placement or fixed salaries. Final hiring decisions rest solely with prospective employers based on individual performance and merit.

verified Cryptographic Digital Certificates

All verified certificates issued by Pragvix Learn carry a unique Certificate ID and a cryptographic QR hash registered under SehatGraph Technologies Private Limited. Employers and academic institutions can verify certificate authenticity online in real time.

security Privacy & DPDP Compliance

Student data, phone numbers, and academic records are stored with high-level encryption in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. We strictly do not monetize, sell, or disclose personal details to external marketing agencies.

code Hands-on Capstone Evaluation

Each track includes real-world capstone assignments that are code-reviewed by our engineering mentors. Passing evaluations ensures that your portfolio meets current tech industry hiring benchmarks.
